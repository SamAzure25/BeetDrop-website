OUR STORY 01

Why BeetDrop exists.

Hospitality is about people. But the experience of discovering, connecting with and managing hospitality services can often feel disconnected.

Guests may have to search across different platforms to find what they need, while businesses often rely on multiple systems to manage their services and connect with their customers.

BeetDrop was created to bring these experiences closer together.

+
One connected experience. Built around people, businesses and hospitality.
OUR MISSION

What we're trying to change.

Our mission is to create a simpler way for people to discover hospitality services, connect with businesses and enjoy better experiences.

We believe technology should remove unnecessary complexity rather than create more of it.
