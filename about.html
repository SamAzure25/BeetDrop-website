01 WHY BEETDROP EXISTS

Built from real experience.

BeetDrop was created from firsthand hospitality experience and an understanding of what happens when operational communication breaks down.

Missed guest requests, inconsistent handovers, disconnected departments and unresolved complaints do not exist as isolated problems — they affect the entire guest experience.

BeetDrop brings reservations, guest communication and operational workflows into one shared environment so teams can work with greater visibility and accountability.

The platform is deliberately being built around practical hospitality workflows rather than unnecessary complexity.

Practical technology.

Designed around the way hospitality teams actually work.

02 — OUR MISSION

Helping hospitality operate smarter.

To help hospitality businesses operate smarter, communicate better and resolve guest issues before they become negative experiences or reviews.

BeetDrop exists to improve communication, accountability and operational visibility while giving hospitality teams practical technology that supports the way they work.

01

THE PERSON BEHIND THE VISION

Ragheema Abrahams

BeetDrop was founded by Ragheema Abrahams, a hospitality professional with hands-on experience across hotel and food-service operations.

Working within hospitality exposed recurring operational challenges including communication gaps between departments, missed guest information, inconsistent handovers and issues that could have been resolved before becoming poor guest experiences.

That experience shaped BeetDrop from the beginning.

Ragheema continues to guide the platform from an operational perspective, ensuring its development remains focused on practical hospitality needs, intuitive workflows and problems that genuinely exist within the industry.
